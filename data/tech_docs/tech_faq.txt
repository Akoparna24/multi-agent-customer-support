Q: I can't log in. What should I do?
A: First check that you are using the email address you signed up with and that Caps Lock is off. If you see "invalid password", use Forgot password on the login screen to set a new one. If you signed up with Google, use the Continue with Google button instead of a password. Clearing the app cache or using an incognito window in the browser also fixes many login problems.

Q: I'm not receiving the OTP or password reset email.
A: OTPs and reset emails usually arrive within 2 minutes. Check your spam and promotions folders and make sure the email address is correct. For SMS OTPs, check that your phone has signal and that SMS from unknown senders is not blocked. You can request a new code after 60 seconds, up to 5 times per hour.

Q: My password reset link says it has expired.
A: Reset links are valid for 30 minutes and can be used only once. Opening the link in a different browser or email app preview can also mark it as used. Request a new link from the login screen and open it directly in the browser where you want to log in.

Q: My account is locked after too many login attempts.
A: After 10 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your password to unlock it immediately. If you did not make these attempts, reset your password and turn on two-factor authentication.

Q: My two-factor authentication code is rejected.
A: Authenticator codes depend on the time on your phone. Turn on automatic date and time in your phone's settings and try a fresh code. If you lost access to your authenticator app, use one of the backup codes you saved when you turned on 2FA. If you have no backup codes, contact technical support to verify your identity.

Q: I think my account was hacked.
A: Reset your password immediately from the login screen and sign out of all devices in Settings > Security. Then turn on two-factor authentication. If you cannot get into your account because the email or password was changed, contact technical support urgently with your account email and the date you last logged in.

Q: The app keeps crashing or closing.
A: Update the app to the latest version from the App Store or Play Store, then restart your phone. If it still crashes, clear the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > CloudNest > Storage > Clear cache), or reinstall the app; your files are stored in the cloud and will not be lost. If a specific screen crashes every time, report it with your phone model and app version.

Q: The app is slow or pages take a long time to load.
A: Slowness is usually caused by a weak internet connection, low phone storage, or an outdated app. Check your connection, free up at least 1 GB of storage, and update the app. In the browser, try disabling extensions or clearing the cache. If the problem started after an update, check our status page for known issues.

Q: The app is stuck on the loading screen or shows a blank white screen.
A: Force close the app and open it again. If it is still stuck, check your internet connection and update the app. On desktop, a blank white screen is often fixed by clearing the browser cache or updating your graphics drivers. If nothing works, reinstall the app.

Q: What do error codes 401, 404, 500 and 503 mean?
A: 401 means your session has expired; log out and log in again. 404 means the page or file no longer exists or the link is outdated. 500 means something went wrong on our side; try again in a few minutes. 503 means the service is temporarily overloaded or under maintenance; check the status page and try again later.

Q: I keep getting logged out or see "session expired".
A: Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity, or immediately if you change your password or sign out of all devices. If you are logged out every few minutes, your browser may be blocking cookies; allow cookies for cloudnest.in. On the app, make sure the date and time on your phone are set automatically.

Q: My files are not syncing between devices.
A: Make sure you are logged in with the same account on all devices and that sync is turned on in Settings > Sync. Sync pauses on battery saver mode and on mobile data if "Sync on Wi-Fi only" is enabled. Files larger than your plan's upload limit will not sync. Pull down on the file list to force a refresh.

Q: Why do my uploads fail?
A: The maximum file size is 100 MB on the Free plan and 10 GB on paid plans. Uploads can also fail on unstable connections; the app retries automatically up to 3 times. If an upload is stuck at 99%, the file is being processed; wait a few minutes before retrying. Check that you have free storage left in your plan.

Q: My files or drafts disappeared.
A: Deleted files are kept in Trash for 30 days and can be restored from there. Drafts are saved automatically every 10 seconds when you are online. If files disappeared after a sync, check the Version history of the folder. Contact technical support if you cannot find them; we can restore files deleted in the last 30 days.

Q: Notifications are not working or arrive twice.
A: Check that notifications are allowed for CloudNest in your phone's settings and in Settings > Notifications in the app. Battery saver or "Do not disturb" can block them. Duplicate notifications usually happen when you are logged in on the same device twice; sign out and sign back in to fix it.

Q: The app won't install or says my device is not compatible.
A: The CloudNest app needs Android 10 or later, or iOS 16 or later, and at least 500 MB of free space. If your device is older, you can still use CloudNest in a web browser at cloudnest.in.

Q: Which browsers are supported?
A: CloudNest works on the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. If a page does not load properly, update your browser, clear its cache, and disable extensions such as ad blockers for cloudnest.in.
